01 / Overview

Getting into high-power rocketry.

I joined AeroMAVS to get more hands-on experience with aerospace engineering. This project is part of my path toward Level 1 certification, and has given me an opportunity to work with rocket CAD and flight simulation.

02 / Design

From CAD to simulation.

I modeled individual components in SolidWorks and brought them together into a full rocket assembly. I also worked with OpenRocket to explore the design and its predicted flight behavior.

SolidWorksPart and assembly modeling
OpenRocketFlight simulation
03 / Current progress

Still in the works.

I've completed initial CAD modeling and simulation work. The rocket is still in development, and my next goal is to continue toward fabrication and the certification flight.
